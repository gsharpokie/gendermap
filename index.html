Cultural Variation in Gender

By Gwen Sharp · Part of the instructor resources for A Sociology Experiment · Updated November 2026

Societies past and present, including the United States and Europe, have organized sex and gender in many different ways. Some recognize more than two genders. Some let people take on another gender’s role, sometimes for a while and sometimes for life. Others define who counts as a man or a woman by work, status, or law rather than by bodies alone. About this map

The cases cover different things: separate gender categories, temporary or lifelong role changes, intersex variation, ideas about what makes someone a man or a woman, and laws. Each entry is a short summary; see its sources for the full picture. Terms and practices change over time, and people within a group don’t all understand them the same way.
